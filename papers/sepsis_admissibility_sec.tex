\section{New-source action-support metadata supplies a semantic oracle}
A frozen validation unit reads the released ICU-Sepsis admissible-action metadata alongside its transition, reward, initial and expert tables from a fresh public checkout at commit \texttt{c9a6d19b85d34883304afaf7cbe1ff02ede9e12b}. This is new public source metadata relative to the saved policy grids, not new patient trajectories. The upstream specification states that inadmissible rows are means over admissible transitions; such means cannot exceed the best supported Bellman action. Sources are \url{https://github.com/icu-sepsis/icu-sepsis} and \url{https://arxiv.org/html/2406.05646v2}. The preregistration SHA256 is \path{96fb96db8f015491e5d83878f5af202d10dab8af41616fb6f34a4ddafbbe8c67}.

Among 17,825 transient state/action rows, 15,587 are inadmissible according to this metadata, or 87.4446 percent. There are 237 transient states with only one admissible action. Every inadmissible row matches its state's mean admissible transition row: maximum absolute residual $7.22\times10^{-16}$, zero rows above the frozen $10^{-12}$ threshold. Support-list lengths, ranges, duplicates, stochastic rows and arrival-reward terminal conventions are checked before solving. Source and file hashes are retained without redistributing the transition archive.

Full-action and masked admissible-action Bellman maxima both converge in 264 iterations with residuals about $9.30\times10^{-11}$. Initial optimal returns are .8751416994800841 and .8751416994800835, and maximum state-value difference is $2.78\times10^{-15}$. Full-action uniform and admissible-uniform returns are .7800709044652784 and .7800709044652779, with maximum state difference $3.00\times10^{-15}$. These equalities validate released mean-extension semantics within numerical precision, not observed treatment efficacy.

A deterministic full-action argmax still names an inadmissible action in 118 transient states. The full-action maximum differs from the best supported Q value by at most $2.00\times10^{-15}$. Equivalent mean rows, ties and numerical ordering can change the selected action identifier without changing the model optimum. This is not a better unsupported treatment: a redundant representation is different from a supported intervention. The source-estimation admissibility threshold itself does not certify patient safety.

Plan, runner and full output are \path{src/sepsis_admissibility_plan.json}, \path{src/sepsis_admissibility_audit.py} and \path{results/sepsis_admissibility_audit.json}. Tests check metadata guards and a separate synthetic absorbing MDP; the pinned source runner is replayed from the clean committed checkout. The ranking and three deferred cross-source candidates are in \path{research/distinct_validation_backlog.md}. This is an external source-level oracle, not an independent clinical cohort or learned-policy benefit. No source-data license grant or patient-data redistribution is implied, and the content-page gate remains uncertified.
